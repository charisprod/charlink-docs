- In Vercel, you can set this by going to your project, `Settings` and finally [Environments Variables](https://vercel.com/docs/concepts/projects/environment-variables). To get GA 4 code `G-ZXX0000XXX` follow [these steps ](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9304153?hl=en).
