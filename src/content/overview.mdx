`CharLink` is a perfect custom landing page for your links on the world wide web.

Usually, you don't need to worry a lot about coding if you're just updating information in `next-seo.config.js`, `BioData.js` and `LinkData.js`. To deep dive know more  [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/docs) and [React](https://react.dev/learn) official documentation.

# Powerful Features

<Feedback marginY="l" variant="success">

1. Harness the full power of [Charis Web Services](https://charisprod.xyz) to show Discord Status.

2. A fresh [Spotify Github Profile](https://github.com/kittinan/spotify-github-profile) for something new. Edit your content and appearance within [Spotify Status](/get-started/frontend#add-spotify-status).

3. Thanks to [bsky-embed](https://github.com/Vincenius/bsky-embed) and [react-social-media-embed](https://github.com/justinmahar/react-social-media-embed).
